First release of the timestamped Tezuka firmware for the **HamGeek Fishball Pluto+ (Zynq 7010 + AD9363)**.

> ⚠️ Fishball Zynq **7010** only. Do not flash on PlutoSDR, Pluto+, LibreSDR or 7020 boards.

### SD image (`tezuka-fishball-ts.zip`)
- Tezuka fishball v0.3.24 (F5OEO), stock kernel and rootfs.
- `system_top.bin`: F5OEO maia-sdr (`refactor` branch) + Phil Greenland's **RX/TX sample timestamping**, Maia kept.
- `sdr_ip_gadget`: fast timestamped UDP sample path over gigabit Ethernet (`direct=1`).
- **Eco mode** (on by default): web UI on demand (`webui on`), AD9363 asleep when no client is connected (wakes up on connection, +~1 s), TX oscillator off when idle. Zynq 77 °C → 65 °C at idle.
- `devicetree.dtb`: TX attenuation at maximum (89.75 dB) at boot; original kept as `devicetree_orig.dtb`.
- `system_top_tezuka.bin`: stock Tezuka bitstream, to go back (replace `system_top.bin`).

Install: copy the zip contents to the root of a FAT32 microSD card. The board uses DHCP; serial console on the FT2232 USB port (115200, `root` / `analog`). Recommended: `fw_setenv mode 1r1t; reboot`.

### Tested
- `Timestamps: YES` (RX/TX) with timestamped SoapyPlutoSDR, gigabit link.
- Timestamped TX→RX loopback (`gsm test-ts`): 9,999/9,999 RX blocks at exactly 1 ms (1R1T + eco mode).
- RX over the network: ~7.4 MS/s CS16 with timestamps. FM reception identical to the stock bitstream.

### Also in the repo
- `srsran/` (4G) and `gsm/` (2G, Osmocom stack installed but not configured) Docker images.
- `firmware/kernel-cpufreq/`: cpufreq kernel experiment (works, no measurable gain, not used).

### Known limitations
- TX→RX loopback latency is constant within a session but varies between sessions (≈ 70–136 samples).
- Without `direct=1` the driver goes through iiod: no timestamps.
