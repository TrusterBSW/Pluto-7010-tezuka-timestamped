Maintenance release of the timestamped Tezuka firmware for the **HamGeek Fishball Pluto+ (Zynq 7010 + AD9363)**.

### Changes since v0.1.0
- **Eco mode: orphan stream watchdog.** If a client is killed without stopping its stream (e.g. srsRAN interrupted), `sdr_ip_gadget` kept sending RX samples forever (up to 45 MB/s, 50 % CPU) and the radio never went back to sleep. The eco daemon now restarts `sdr_ip_gadget` after 2 minutes of RX DMA activity with no iiod client (room for a temporary network outage) (`ORPH_DELAI` in `eco.conf`). Verified: stream stopped, radio asleep 30 s later.
- **srsRAN Docker image:** fix device discovery with `hostname=` (Phil's SoapyPlutoSDR only tried the hostname after a successful Avahi scan, which fails in containers, so srsRAN found no device). `PRB=50` accepted (experimental).

### Measurements (2026-11-08)
- srsRAN 4G over gigabit Ethernet: 6, 15 and 25 PRB run without RF errors; board CPU ~90 % at 25 PRB. 50 PRB does not work.
- Max timestamped RX rate: 8.3 MS/s stock, 11.4 MS/s with Tezuka's CPU950/DDR600 overclock FSBL (out of spec, +4–5 °C, not enabled).
- CPU frequency scaling and DDR3 power-down during sleep: no measurable temperature gain (not used).
